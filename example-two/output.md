# Response: This Is Not a Follow-Up Situation

Source: [case.md](case.md).

## Why This Tool Does Not Apply Here

Rhea has replied. This is exactly the situation [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md)'s own stop condition names: "a reply is already sitting unanswered from them; that is not a follow-up situation, it is a reply situation." Running the decide-the-next-move logic on this, follow up now, wait, close the loop, would treat a live conversation as though it had gone quiet, when it has not.

## What Actually Needs to Happen

Respond to what she actually said: acknowledge the bandwidth concern directly, confirm that letting us know closer to the date works fine, and do not push for a firmer commitment now than she has actually offered. This is a straightforward reply to a real message, not a decision about whether, or what, to follow up with.

## What This Tests

Whether the tool correctly recognises when a situation has already moved past "gone quiet" into an actual live reply, rather than mechanically applying its own decision tree to every case that mentions someone who has not yet fully committed.
