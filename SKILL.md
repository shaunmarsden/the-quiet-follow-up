# The Quiet Follow-Up

You do not need to install anything to try this once: copy this whole file, paste it as your first message in any AI chat tool, then follow it with your actual inputs.

A follow-up is a decision, not a template applied on a timer. Before drafting anything, work out what actually happened: has this gone quiet because of genuine disinterest, a change of circumstance, a busy period, or an unanswered question sitting in the way? Each calls for something different, and some call for not following up at all.

## Gather the Inputs

- What was originally said or sent, and what mattered to the other person about it
- What has been sent already, and when
- Anything since the last message: an automated reply, a stated change of circumstance, a specific question left unanswered, silence with no signal either way
- How many follow-ups, if any, have already gone out

## Decide the Next Move

Before drafting, choose one:

- **Follow up now**: enough time has passed and there is a genuine reason to reach out
- **Wait**: not enough time has passed, or a known reason (a holiday period, a stated busy stretch) makes now a bad time
- **Change the contact**: this person has gone quiet in a way that suggests they are not the right person to hear back from; identify who else might be
- **Answer something first**: a specific question or concern was left unaddressed; answering it is worth more than a plain nudge
- **Reframe**: the original message did not land; a different, specific angle, still anchored to what actually matters to them, might
- **Close the loop**: enough silence has passed that continuing to follow up would look worse than a clean close-out
- **Stop**: there is a clear signal, an explicit decline, a stated change that rules this out, that this is not worth pursuing further

Only draft a message once one of these has actually been decided. Do not default to "send the next one in the sequence" without this step.

## Anchor Every Message to the Other Person

Every message must be anchored to something real and specific to them, never to your own schedule or agenda. Never open with "just checking in", "just circling back", "hope you're well" or "I wanted to follow up." These read as filler to anyone who receives this kind of message often.

Weak anchor: "Just following up on my last message."
Strong anchor: a direct reference to the specific thing they said mattered, or the specific question left open, stated as though it still matters, because it does.

## Apply the Guardrails

- No stage sends automatically because time has passed; each one restates the decision above
- If the other person replies at any point, drop the sequence and respond to what they actually said
- Do not remind them, in an early or middle message, that previous messages were already sent; that reads as pressure about your own process, not something useful to them. A deliberate close-out is the one exception, acknowledging the full sequence honestly is exactly its job there

## Stop When the Task Is Unsafe

Do not draft a follow-up when:

- There is a clear signal of an explicit decline or a stated change that rules this out
- The only available anchor is a restatement of your own schedule, with nothing real from their side
- A reply is already sitting unanswered from them; that is not a follow-up situation, it is a reply situation

## Require Human Review

This proposes what to send and why, or that nothing should be sent. Sending it stays subject to explicit human approval.
