# Fictional Cases: Bramble Hill Food Bank Volunteer Sign-Ups

An entirely fictional community food bank, following up with three people who signed up for a Saturday sorting shift and have gone quiet since.

## Case A: Dev Malhotra

Signed up two weeks ago via the online form. No confirmation reply since, no other contact. No stated reason either way.

## Case B: Alicia Ferreira

Signed up, then emailed asking: "Is there parking on site, or should I get the bus? Just want to plan my morning." No answer was ever sent back to her.

## Case C: Owen Bishop

Signed up, then emailed a week later: "Actually, sorry, I don't think I can commit to this after all, my Saturdays have got busier than expected."
